## What is Numwee?

Numwee is a math game where you raise weird little pets, beat boss monsters, and accidentally learn algebra along the way. Five modes ramp from cozy counting all the way up to logarithms — and somewhere in there you're hatching dragons, taming a smart purple beast, and chasing chests full of golden numbers.

No ads. No tracking. Works offline. One small purchase unlocks the boss modes and rare pets — forever, no subscriptions, no tricks.

**Free forever:**

- 🪺 Cozy Corner — relaxed practice, no timer
- 🐾 Tiny Paws — counting, comparing, simple patterns
- 🗺️ Treasure Trail — sequences, number bonds, early arithmetic
- 🐰 11 Common pet species

**Numwee Plus (one-time, $4.99):**

- 🐉 Dragon Quest — times tables, fractions, percentages
- 🧠 Brainy Beast — algebra, primes, square roots, exponents, logs
- ✨ 17 Rare, Epic, Mythical, and Celestial pet species
- 🎁 All future content added to these modes and pets
