# Numwee Privacy Policy

*Effective Date: June 10, 2026*
*Last Updated: June 10, 2026*

## The short version

**Numwee collects no data from you or your child.** No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no third-party trackers. Everything stays on the device. We have no way to identify, contact, or target individual users.

If you stop reading here, you've understood our policy.

---

## What data Numwee collects

**None.** Numwee does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, behavioral data, or identifiers from users.

Specifically, Numwee does not collect:

- Name, email address, phone number, postal address, or other contact information.
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, advertising ID, or hardware serial numbers).
- Location data of any kind.
- Photos, contacts, calendar entries, or any other content from your device.
- Usage analytics, crash reports, performance telemetry, or session recordings.
- Demographic information or behavioral inferences.

Your child's game progress — pet collection, scores, day streak, daily quest, leaderboards — is stored **only on your iOS device** using Apple's local storage APIs (`UserDefaults`). We never receive, see, or have access to any of this data.

## In-app purchases

Numwee offers one optional in-app purchase: Numwee Plus ($4.99 USD, one-time). The transaction is processed entirely by Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and reports back to our app only whether the purchase succeeded. We never see your payment information, Apple ID, billing address, or any other transaction detail.

If you have a billing question, contact [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com).

## Third-party services

Numwee uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, social media integrations, or external services. There are no third-party services involved in the operation of the app.

The background music tracks in Numwee are CC0-licensed (royalty-free) from Pixabay. Credit is shown in the in-app Settings screen for attribution. We do not transmit any data to Pixabay.

## Children's privacy (COPPA)

Numwee is designed for children. Because Numwee collects no personal information from any user — child or adult — there is no risk of unauthorized collection of children's data. Numwee is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design: there is nothing to collect.

Numwee does not contain links to external websites, social media, advertising, or any other content that could lead a child outside the app, with the sole exception of three small Settings-screen links intended for adults: the Pixabay music attribution and the links to this Privacy Policy and to our Terms of Use.

## Family Sharing

Numwee Plus is enabled for Apple Family Sharing. If a member of your Family Group purchases Numwee Plus, every other member can access it on their device at no additional cost. This is handled entirely by Apple's Family Sharing system; we receive no information about your family group or its members.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever make a material change to this policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and document the change here. Because Numwee currently collects no data, future material changes are unlikely.
